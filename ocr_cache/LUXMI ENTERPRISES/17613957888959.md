[P1-L2] Scope of Work for supply of printers and cartridges on demand basis:

[P1-L3] 1. Supply and Installation of the cartridges as per the table below:

[P1-L6] | 1    | HP 77A Black LaserJet Toner Cartridge - CF277A | 94 | HP Laserjet Pro M329dw |

[P1-L7] Note: Each cartridges will be evaluated based on item wise L1 bidder.

[P1-L8] 2. Delivery for cartridges: Delivery shall be taken as per the requirement of IOCL time to time for three years from the date of successful installation and commissioning of printers. There shall be no quantity committed from IOCL. The bidder has to supply the required items on same rate as quoted in Bid. IOCL will issue demand notice through email against the rate contract for cartridges. Bidder has to supply the cartridges within one week from the date of intimation.

[P1-L9] 3. Vendor should handover/deliver the balance leftover quantity of cartridges in the last quarter of rate contract to IndianOil.

[P1-L10] 4. Bidder to provide escalation matrix for complaint logging and timely resolution. Response time for resolving the complaints is next business-day and includes free telephone support 24*7.

[P1-L11] 5. Payment Terms for Cartridges: Payment shall be made to the vendor for actual number of cartridges supplied in each quarter.

[P1-L12] Required documents:

[P1-L13] The following documents should be submitted by the bidders

[P1-L14] 1. Authorized certificate: Bidder need to submit Manufacturer’s Authorization Form (MAF) from OEM, reference to this bid for “Supply of cartridges.”

[P1-L15] 2. The bidder must submit genuineness certificate along with signed and stamped Manufacturer authorization form (MAF) duly signed by OEM authorized signatory.

[P1-L16] 3. In case any toner is found faulty, the bidder will attend to the same and will arrange for replacement from OEM if required. A declaration in this regard must be submitted by the bidder.
